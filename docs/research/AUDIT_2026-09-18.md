# Scientific + code audit — 2026-09-18

Scope: uploaded project description, panel workbook, 10-subindex workbook, EDA script/notebook, and panel-construction notebook.

## What the current project is doing

The original project asks which PCI component has the strongest effect on enterprise revenue. It builds a 2010–2024 province-year panel, explores correlations/VIF among 10 PCI sub-indices, and proposes Random Forest, XGBoost, and Elastic Net.

That is a useful starting data asset, but the current design conflates three different questions:
1. descriptive correlation;
2. predictive importance;
3. causal institutional effect.

These must be separated before the paper is scientifically defensible.

## Confirmed implementation defects

### A1 — Notebook syntax failure (critical)
`EDA_PCI_10_CSTP.ipynb` contains eight code cells whose first line is an uncommented Vietnamese separator, producing Python syntax errors. The standalone `.py` has the intended comments.
Fix: regenerate the notebook from tested source or convert those separator lines to Markdown cells. Do not maintain two divergent copies of the same logic.

### A2 — Non-portable absolute paths (critical)
Legacy scripts refer to `/mnt/user-data/uploads/` and `/mnt/user-data/outputs/`.
Fix: use repository-relative paths with `pathlib.Path`, configurable from CLI/environment.

### A3 — Manual revenue transcription (critical data risk; partially resolved)
Revenue values were hardcoded/transcribed from GSO/NSO tables/screenshots. DATA-INTEGRITY-01 found three confirmed transcription errors:
- Hòa Bình 2016: 23,040 → 33,040;
- Gia Lai 2023: 133,195 → 131,195;
- An Giang 2023: 212,941 → 212,961.

The correction registry and lineage are now committed. G2 must make these corrections part of the deterministic build rather than relying on a manually edited workbook.

### A4 — Structural missingness could be mishandled
PCI sub-index 6 does not exist in 2010–2012 in the constructed panel.
Fix: treat as unavailable by design; primary 10-subindex sample starts 2013, lagged sample 2014. Do not interpolate.

### A5 — Pooled correlation is not a panel effect
Pearson correlation over all province-years mixes between-province level differences, within-province changes, and national time trends.
Fix: add within-province demeaning / two-way FE estimates and interpret pooled correlation only as EDA.

### A6 — Feature importance is not impact
Random Forest/XGBoost importance can be distorted by correlated features and does not estimate causal effects.
Fix: inferential claims from prespecified econometric models; ML used for out-of-sample prediction and nonlinear pattern discovery.

### A7 — Risk of temporal leakage
Legacy plan says train earlier years/test later years 'or by province'. A random/province-only split is not sufficient for a future-prediction claim.
Fix: fixed expanding/rolling temporal folds; optionally a second geographic generalization test by held-out province/region.

### A8 — Raw revenue scale is problematic
Aggregate provincial revenue is strongly driven by province size, inflation, number of firms, and structural composition.
Fix: use log outcome; add real/deflated and scale-normalized alternatives when possible (e.g., revenue per active enterprise), and control for core province characteristics.

### A9 — COVID/regime break
2020–2021 can alter both business outcomes and governance perceptions.
Fix: year FE plus explicit robustness excluding/isolating COVID years and potential interactions.

### A10 — Hardcoded plotting assumptions
Legacy EDA hardcodes sample-size text and creates a triangular mask that is not actually applied to the heatmap.
Fix: compute labels from data and either apply/remove the mask consistently.

### A11 — Trend visualization drops valid early data
Trend plots based only on the complete 10-subindex sample discard 2010–2012 values for the nine sub-indices that do exist.
Fix: show available-data trends 2010–2024 for nine indices, clearly marking sub-index 6 from 2013.

## DATA-INTEGRITY-01 resolution

The legacy notebook recorded three large region subtotal-vs-province-sum discrepancies and initially left open the possibility that they reflected published-source anomalies. That interpretation is superseded.

Direct re-reading of the archived revenue tables plus official NSO cross-checks found three province-level transcription errors. After correction:
- the prior -10,000 northern-region mismatch is eliminated;
- the Tây Nguyên 2023 residual becomes +1;
- the Mekong 2023 residual becomes -1;
- among all 90 region-year subtotal checks, maximum absolute residual is 3.

Therefore, do not describe the three large mismatches as GSO/NSO source errors in the manuscript. They were data-entry errors in the derived project panel.

## Literature conflict that the paper must confront

Recent Vietnam evidence is not unanimous. Some studies report positive links between institutional quality and firm performance/productivity, while a 2026 ADB working paper reports weak or limited effects on several provincial manufacturing outcomes. This disagreement is a research opportunity: the project should test stability rather than assume PCI must have a strong positive revenue effect.

## Revised contribution

Recommended paper contribution:

> A transparent province-panel study testing whether individual PCI dimensions contain robust, forward-looking explanatory and predictive signal for enterprise revenue outcomes, comparing two-way fixed-effects inference with time-respecting machine-learning benchmarks and explicitly testing stability across outcomes and periods.

This is stronger than 'run three ML models and rank feature importance' because the contribution becomes scientific design, reproducibility, and robust decision relevance.
