## Deadline
Online registration: 01/09/2026 through 25/09/2026.

## Field
The project fits the Economics field, including economics/development economics and related business/economic research.

## Scoring structure (100 points)
- Purpose and significance: 15.
- Scientific/professional content and research methodology: 30.
- Novelty and creativity: 30.
- Practical application / expandability / valuable recommendations: 15.
- Presentation and specific citations: 10.

Project implication: method + novelty = 60 points. A sophisticated ML model does not create novelty by itself; novelty must be tied to a defensible question, better identification/evaluation, and useful decision insight.

## Required research structure
Cover, table of contents, lists, abstract, problem statement, literature review, objectives/methods, results/discussion, conclusion/recommendations, references, appendices.

## Formatting / anonymity
- A4.
- Times New Roman, size 13.
- Line spacing 1.3–1.5.
- Left margin 3 cm; top/bottom/right 2 cm.
- No author name, school name/logo, supervisor name, thanks, signature, or identifying marks in the research content, including appendices/surveys.
- Vietnamese or English.

## Poster
Poster required for registration; official rules specify portrait 0.8 m × 1.3 m.

## Recent Euréka signal scan (2021–2025)

The official site exposes proceedings for 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025. Searchable/indexed access varies by year, so conclusions below use the official archive plus indexed proceedings/award records and are used only as competition-orientation evidence, not as a substitute for the scientific literature review.

Observed pattern:
- Economics finalists repeatedly combine a concrete applied problem with explicit quantitative evidence and actionable recommendations.
- Advanced methods are present, including machine learning/deep learning, but successful work is framed around a real economic decision rather than 'using AI' as the contribution.
- Institutional quality/local growth is already represented: the 2022 proceedings include 'Chất lượng thể chế, phân cấp tài khóa và tăng trưởng địa phương: Phân tích đa chiều'. Therefore a generic 'PCI affects revenue' paper is not novel enough by itself.
- 2023 Economics first prize examined global value-chain participation and firms' digital transformation using international evidence; the contribution was substantive, not just algorithmic.
- 2024 Economics first prize used deep learning for portfolio optimization in Vietnam, showing the competition accepts advanced computational work when the method matches the economic problem.
- 2025 award records show economics work spanning policy, forecasting, networks, and optimization, again emphasizing application and modern quantitative methods.

## Strategy for this project

To align with the rubric, emphasize:
1. A sharper scientific question: which dimensions of local economic governance contain robust forward-looking signal for enterprise outcomes after province and year heterogeneity are controlled?
2. Methodological novelty: combine interpretable panel inference with strictly time-respecting ML prediction and stability analysis.
3. Practical output: province-specific diagnostic dashboard / policy prioritization framework only after robustness is established.
4. Transparency: data lineage, open reproducible checks, and explicit limits on causal interpretation.
