## 4. G3 statistical baseline result

G3 STAT-BASELINE-01 passed on the prespecified 2014–2024 sample (693 observations, 63 province clusters).

Model:
- 10 standardized one-year-lagged PCI components;
- province fixed effects + year fixed effects;
- province-clustered standard errors;
- cluster-t inference with small-sample/df correction;
- Benjamini–Hochberg FDR across the 10 component coefficients per model/outcome.

Primary findings:
- **Log revenue level:** no PCI component has BH-adjusted q < 0.05.
- **Log revenue change:** CSTP5 (Chi phí không chính thức) is the only component with BH-adjusted q < 0.05:
  - beta = 0.03628 per 1 SD;
  - 95% CI [0.01852, 0.05404];
  - p = 0.000129;
  - BH q = 0.001289;
  - exp(beta)-1 ≈ 3.69%.
- Excluding outcome years 2020–2021: CSTP5 remains positive, beta = 0.03840, q = 0.01290.
- Leave-one-province-out for the full log-change model: CSTP5 beta range [0.03403, 0.03947], 0/63 sign flips.

Interpretation is strictly associational. This does not establish that CSTP5 causally changes revenue and does not justify calling it the “most impactful” component.

## 5. Primary unresolved risks

1. Nominal aggregate provincial revenue reflects province size, inflation, sector mix, and number of firms.
2. Two-way FE does not eliminate reverse causality or time-varying omitted-variable bias.
3. COVID sensitivity in G3 excludes 2020–2021 outcome years; a stricter pandemic-window design remains for G5.
4. Spatial dependence may remain across neighboring provinces.
5. PCI construction and economic structure may drift over the long panel.
6. Exact originating publication/page for the archived 2020–2024 Table 151 extract remains a documented lineage caveat.
