## 1. Research objective

Study whether and how the 10 PCI sub-indices are associated with subsequent enterprise outcomes at province-year level in Vietnam, and whether those governance dimensions add genuine future-year predictive value beyond strong non-PCI baselines.

Use association / relationship / predictive signal language. No causal claim is supported by the current design.

## 4. G3 inference result

Prespecified sample: 2014–2024, N=693, 63 province clusters.

Model:
- 10 standardized lagged PCI components;
- province FE + year FE;
- province-clustered cluster-t inference;
- BH FDR across ten component tests.

Findings:
- log revenue level: no component has BH q<0.05;
- log revenue change: CSTP5 is the only BH-adjusted component in the primary model:
  - beta = 0.03628;
  - 95% CI [0.01852, 0.05404];
  - p = 0.000129;
  - q = 0.001289;
  - exp(beta)-1 ≈ 3.69%.

This is conditional association, not causal effect.

## 5. G4 prediction result

Six fixed expanding outer folds: test years 2019–2024. All preprocessing/tuning is training-period only.

Primary log-change mean MAE:
- Elastic Net non-PCI: **0.08819** (best);
- Elastic Net + PCI: 0.08890;
- Random Forest + PCI: 0.09523;
- XGBoost non-PCI: 0.10172;
- XGBoost + PCI: 0.10321;
- Random Forest non-PCI: 0.10365;
- naive: 0.11484.

RF gains ~8.12% versus RF without PCI and wins 5/6 same-family folds, but remains ~7.98% worse than the stronger non-PCI Elastic Net and beats it only 1/6 folds.

For log revenue level, non-PCI Elastic Net is also best (mean MAE 0.08859). PCI worsens mean MAE for all three model families.

No PCI-added model passes the preregistered feature-importance gate; therefore no SHAP/permutation ranking is reported.

## 6. G5 robustness result

CSTP5 primary lagged association:
- survives 1% winsorization: beta=0.03416, q=0.001491;
- leave-one-year beta range [0.03191, 0.04924], 0 sign flips;
- leave-one-region beta range [0.02907, 0.03990], 0 sign flips;
- lag 1 remains positive in distributed-lag model; lag 2 is null;
- future-lead placebo is null (p=0.47656).

But the signal is bounded:
- strict exclusion of outcome years 2020–2022: beta=0.03316, q=0.06720;
- contemporaneous 10-component model: CSTP5 p=0.99419;
- longer 2011–2024 nine-component model: CSTP5 q=0.31121.

Pesaran CD on full-change residuals: p=0.166; short T limits interpretation.

## 7. Integrated scientific conclusion

The project does **not** support a universal PCI-component ranking.

Defensible conclusion:
- CSTP5 shows a lagged, specification-bounded association with subsequent revenue change in the primary FE model;
- the relationship is not stable across every period/specification;
- PCI does not add stable future-year predictive value beyond the strongest non-PCI regularized baseline;
- a statistically detectable panel association need not imply incremental forecasting value or causal impact.

This distinction between inference, prediction and falsification is the main contribution.

## 9. Remaining risks

1. Aggregate nominal revenue embeds province scale, firm count and sector composition.
2. Two-way FE does not remove time-varying confounding/reverse causality.
3. Scale-normalized outcomes require new verified denominator data.
4. Spatial mechanisms are not ruled out.
5. PCI measurement can drift across the long period.
6. Exact publication/page for archived 2020–2024 Table 151 remains a documented lineage caveat.
