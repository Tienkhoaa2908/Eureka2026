## 1. Research objective

Study whether and how the 10 PCI sub-indices are associated with subsequent enterprise outcomes at the province-year level in Vietnam, and test whether nonlinear machine-learning models add out-of-sample predictive value beyond interpretable panel-econometric baselines.

Until a defensible identification strategy is established, use the words association, relationship, predictive signal, or out-of-sample prediction. Do not claim causal impact.

## 4. Current scientific design

Primary inferential specification:

- sample: 2014–2024, 693 province-year rows;
- outcome 1: log enterprise net revenue;
- outcome 2 robustness: annual log revenue change / revenue growth formulation documented before estimation;
- predictors: standardized one-year-lagged PCI sub-indices;
- province fixed effects + year fixed effects;
- standard errors clustered by province;
- report coefficient, confidence interval, and uncertainty; no causal wording.

Prediction track remains downstream:

- Elastic Net, Random Forest, XGBoost;
- expanding/rolling time splits only;
- compare against naive/linear baselines;
- feature importance only after out-of-sample value is demonstrated.

## 5. Primary unresolved risks

1. Outcome validity: nominal aggregate provincial revenue is affected by province size, inflation, sector mix, and number of firms.
2. Identification: two-way FE does not by itself solve reverse causality or time-varying omitted variables.
3. Methodology drift: PCI construction and local economic structure can change across the long panel.
4. COVID/regime break: 2020–2021 requires explicit sensitivity analysis.
5. Spatial dependence may remain across neighboring provinces.
6. Exact originating publication/page for the archived 2020–2024 Table 151 extract remains a documented lineage caveat.
